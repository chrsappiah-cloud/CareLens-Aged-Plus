CareLens Aged+ is clinical decision-support for aged care professionals.

Sign in with the demo administrator account provided. On the login screen, tap "Admin: admin@carelens.health" to autofill credentials, then tap Sign In.

After login you can explore:
• Dashboard — facility overview and risk summary
• Clients — sample client profiles with assessments
• Intake — 7-step biopsychosocial workflow
• Assess — NeuroWatch and assessment modules
• Care Plan / Reports — sample care documentation
• Settings — Privacy Policy, Terms, subscription management

Subscriptions use StoreKit 2. In-app purchases are optional; the demo admin account has Enterprise access without purchase.

AI features generate decision-support text only — not diagnoses. A disclaimer is shown in assessment and insight screens.

No special hardware required. CloudKit sync uses the reviewer's Apple ID sandbox environment.
