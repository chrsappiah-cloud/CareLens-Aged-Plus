CareLens Aged+ is an intelligent aged care platform for clinicians, facility managers, and care teams. Deliver safer, more proactive care with structured assessments, NeuroWatch monitoring, and AI-assisted clinical decision support.

KEY FEATURES

• NeuroWatch Engine — Track cognitive trends and receive early risk alerts with actionable clinical guidance.

• Biopsychosocial Assessments — Complete structured intake workflows including medical history, cognitive screening, functional assessment, social support, and risk factors.

• AI Clinical Insights — Generate differential considerations, care plan suggestions, and report narratives using on-device and cloud clinical analysis. All outputs are decision-support only and require professional review.

• Client Management — Maintain longitudinal client profiles with assessments, incidents, and collateral reports.

• Care Plans & Reports — Build evidence-based care plans and export clinical summaries for multidisciplinary teams.

• Monitoring & Charts — Visualise longitudinal data to spot changes between reviews.

• Secure Sync — CloudKit sync with optional encrypted backup. Role-based access for administrators, clinicians, and carers.

• Role-Based Access — Free, Starter, Professional, and Enterprise tiers assigned by your organisation administrator.

IMPORTANT

CareLens Aged+ is clinical decision-support software. It does not provide emergency services or replace qualified medical judgement. Always verify recommendations before acting on them.
